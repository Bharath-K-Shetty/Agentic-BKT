How to interpret the result

The network result uses current balance, fee quotation and an unsigned simulation. It is not signed preflight and cannot guarantee later success.

The ML values are actual trained-model outputs from fresh sampled history. They are experimental scores, not validated live failure probabilities. Sparse coverage, different networks and changing conditions can make them unreliable. The model uses account roles and history, not the transfer amount. Balance checks and simulation handle amount-specific problems. No transfer is blocked because of ML.

Receipt verification requires the exact checked message and an outcome after the recorded check. Missing receipts remain unknown. Fees paid are measured from receipts; fees saved and time saved remain unproven.
