How it works

A new wallet has no history. The accounts it touches do.

STEP 1

A transaction is about to be sent

Often from a wallet with no past results to learn from.

STEP 2

HOMIE checks the accounts it touches

How often transactions using those accounts failed, using only results at least an hour old.

STEP 3

A risk score, before sending

High-risk transactions get a warning and a re-check, so good ones still go through.
