Does stopping a failed attempt avoid a fee?

We ran real transactions on a local Solana test validator. Two wallets started with equal balances and tried the same tiny transfer to separate unfunded accounts. The recipient’s minimum balance requirement made the transfer fail.

What this proves

The failed on-chain transaction has a confirmed receipt. Its fee matches the wallet’s balance loss; the recipient received nothing. The matching checked attempt was stopped and its balance stayed unchanged.

What it means for our project

Checking before sending can avoid a paid failed attempt. Standard Solana preflight caught this example for free too. This experiment does not show an improvement over preflight, ML-driven fee savings, mainnet savings or time saved.

Reproduce the experiment
From the project root, with the Solana CLI and Python dependencies installed:
.venv/bin/python research/solana/fee_proof/run.py
Starts its own isolated validator, creates throwaway wallets, checks and submits test transactions, verifies receipts and balances, saves fresh evidence, then stops the validator. Reload this page after completion.
