Check before you send

Check a plain SOL transfer for insufficient balance or a simulation error before making a paid attempt. This checker never signs or submits a transaction.

Transfer details

Public addresses only. Balance and simulation checks use the selected network’s public Solana endpoint. Examples use public addresses without controlling them; their balances can change.

Ready to check

Enter the transfer details. The check will estimate the network fee and look for execution problems.

How this can help

A problem found before submission gives you a chance to fix it before retrying on-chain. That may avoid a wasted attempt and its possible fee. The checker reports the current fee estimate and its own check duration; live fees saved and retry time saved have not been measured. See the measured local fee experiment →

What this check covers

Only a plain native SOL transfer is checked. This is not a token swap, contract audit or malicious-wallet check. A passing simulation does not verify your signature or guarantee later execution. Account minimum balances and changing network state can affect the result.

The live checker uses RPC balance, fee and simulation checks. The historical ML experiment is available in Research replay. Its older account histories are not used to claim a fresh live ML prediction.

Fee estimates are not savings: some failed submissions do not pay the estimated fee. We have not demonstrated superiority over simulation, prevented failures, or money saved.
