How the comparison works

The existing model uses wallet history and read-only account information. Our model adds past outcomes involving accounts the transaction can change. The displayed estimates use a separate calibration follow-up; they are not the paper’s identity-controlled comparison.

The first four examples were chosen to show both correct predictions and mistakes. Other examples follow time order. These examples do not measure overall accuracy. This study covers transactions included in blocks; it has not demonstrated live failure prevention.

Research results and limitations

In the paper’s reserved test, average precision rose from 0.1688 to 0.2004 after an account-identity control. This measures ranking, not accuracy. The 95% interval for the gain is +0.0168 to +0.0501.
